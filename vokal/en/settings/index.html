Microphone

Audio latency

It takes tens of milliseconds for the track to reach your ears and your voice to reach the analysis. Without compensation it looks like you are late. The browser estimates the latency itself (now ≈ 30 ms), but measuring it is more accurate.

Calibration (10 seconds). You will hear 8 clicks. If you sing with headphones, hold an earpiece to the microphone. If the clicks come from speakers, do nothing. You can also clap exactly in time with the clicks, but that is less accurate.

Recorded your voice and it lags behind the track in the studio? The studio editor has Voice and track together: shift the voice by ear or match it to the original notes and press Apply to future recordings — the latency will be saved here.

Notes and scoring

Game

XP, levels and achievements are always counted — only the effects are turned off. All rewards are in Awards.

Separation server (GPU)

Makes the hardest case cleaner — an original without an instrumental: a neural network separates the voice from the music and CREPE detects the notes. Without the server the voice is removed right in the browser (quick mode: approximate notes, a faint echo of the voice in the instrumental). The server runs on a computer with a GPU; everything else works without it.

Not found

Start: project folder → server\Запустить сервер.bat. For the AI coach, put a Claude API key in server\.env (line ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=…). The key stays on the server and never reaches the browser.
