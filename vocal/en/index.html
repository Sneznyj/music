How it works

Three steps to a finished track

1Record or upload

From the microphone (over a backing track if you like, with reverb in your headphones) or a ready file from REAPER, a voice recorder or your phone.

2Choose a sound

A preset — Velvet, Pop Hit, Ballad, Rock, Autotune effect… Compare before and after with one button.

3Download

MP3 or 24-bit WAV, the voice alone or mixed with the track, at streaming loudness.

For those who want control

Fine-tune every module

Switch modules on and off, tweak the parameters and save your own presets. The knobs respond instantly.

Autotune

Follows the song's key with adjustable naturalness: from invisible correction to the T-Pain effect.

Cleanup

Noise reduction from a room profile, a de-esser for harsh "s" sounds, silence between phrases.

Equalizer

Low cut, mud, presence, air — with the curve on screen.

Compressor

Density and speed: an even voice that always sits up front.

Warmth

Tube saturation — a richer sound, and a husky edge if you push it.

Double

A second voice on the sides — wider and richer.

Tempo echo

Slapback, 1/8, 1/4 — in time with the track.

Reverb

Plate, room, hall, cathedral; pre-delay and ducking.

Export

MP3 or 24-bit WAV, −14 LUFS loudness, mixed with the track.

Your voice stays with you

Recording, autotune, effects and analysis run on your device. We do not store your recordings and require no sign-up. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up needed; all presets and exports are available right away.

Where does my voice go?

Nowhere. Recording and processing happen in your browser, on your device. Projects are saved there too — only you can see them.

Do I need headphones?

For recording over a backing track — yes, otherwise the music leaks into the microphone. For a cappella recording and analysing a ready file you do not.

Will a phone microphone do?

Yes. A studio microphone is better, but noise reduction, autotune and reverb noticeably improve a phone recording too.

Which files can I upload?

Anything your browser can open: wav, mp3, m4a, ogg, flac — for example a take from REAPER, FL Studio or a voice recorder.

Can I publish the result?

Your own recordings — yes, they are yours. Publishing covers of other people's songs may require rights — see the terms of use.
